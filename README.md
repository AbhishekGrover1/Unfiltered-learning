### `learning-in-public`

**A raw, unfiltered record of my journey in AI/ML and software engineering.**

No perfect commits.
No polished stories.
Just **experiments, mistakes, debugging, breakthroughs, and progress.**

---

## 🧪 What's Inside

This repository is my personal learning playground.

I use it to document:

* 🐛 Bugs I couldn't figure out
* ❌ Approaches that failed
* 🧠 Concepts I'm trying to understand
* 🔬 Experiments and random ideas
* 🛠️ Small projects and practice work
* 💡 Things I discovered while building
* 🚀 Solutions that finally worked
* 📈 Progress, setbacks, and lessons learned

---

## 🧩 The Philosophy

> **Don't hide the mistakes. Document them.**

Most projects only show the final result.

This repository shows the **process**.

Some days I'll build something useful.
Some days I'll break everything.
Some days I'll spend hours fixing one stupid error.

All of it counts.
